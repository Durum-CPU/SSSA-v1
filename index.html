The Architect
Sebastian S. S. Anthonsen

Hello there! I would like to tell you something about myself.
I am 28 years old and counting . I graduated from high school in 2019 and I have been working ever since.
During those years, I have gained experience from several very different industries and work environments, which has taught me a lot about responsibility, communication, teamwork
and adapting to new situations. Before I started studying IT-Architecture, I spent several years working with people, logistics, production and service.
These experiences have given me a broad perspective on how different workplaces operate and how important it is to understand both people and the systems they work with.
Below is a chronological overview of my educational background:

  1. Christianshavns Døtreskole 2007/13
  2. Ringe Fri- og Efterskole 2013/14
  3. Helsingør Gymnasium 2014-2017
  4. KVUC 2017-2019
  5. IT-Arkitektur 2026-2030

My educational background has taken me through several different environments, and each stage has contributed to where I am today.
I have always been interested in learning new things and understanding how things work, which is one of the reasons I chose to pursue IT-Architecture.
I have had a wide range of work experience.
Here is a short list about some of my experience (Non-chronological):

  • Special needs educator for adult people with disabilities, primarily autism. (6 years)
  • Airplane cleaner and baggage handler in CPH Airport. (1 year)
  • Pharmaceutical factory and factory jobs. (2 years)
  • Baker's apprentice (1.5 years)
  • Catering (1 year)

Working in such different fields has taught me that problems can look very different depending on the environment.
Sometimes the solution is technical, while other times it is about communication, organization or simply understanding the people involved. Now here I am, studying to become an IT Architect.
I am particularly interested in the connection between technology, people and business. I want to understand not only how to build technical solutions,
but also why a solution is needed and how it can create real value.
In the future, I hope to work as an independent consultant, helping businesses understand their challenges, solve problems and develop useful and innovative solutions.
My goal is to combine the experience I have gained from working with people and different industries with the technical knowledge I am developing through my education.
This portfolio is a place where I can document that journey, showcase my projects and demonstrate what I am learning along the way.

This is only the beginning.
